KARTSICK

An original browser kart racer for friends. This build is the Butterbell driving study, not the complete release.

Original art and audio

Kartsick contributors, 2026. Original visual designs, generated artwork, and original music/sound designs are shared under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. Credit the contributors, link the license and source repository, and indicate changes when distributing adaptations.
